## Known limitation

An Earth twin (1 R⊕, 365-day orbit, Sun-like star) is predicted at 9.9 M⊕ (true: 1). Only 0 training planets are within the similarity radius: small planets on long orbits rarely have measured masses, so the model is extrapolating. The game counts similar training planets and warns when there are fewer than 5.
